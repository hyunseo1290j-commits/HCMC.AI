THE SHORT ANSWERS

Do I need my own AI provider key?＋

No. hcmc.ai is designed as a consumer studio. Provider credentials stay on the protected server and users work with plans or tokens.

Why does the price change between models?＋

Providers charge different rates by model, duration, resolution and audio. The live quote calculates the exact selected combination before you generate.

Which model should I start with?＋

Use the Models comparison or start with a template. The studio only shows settings that the selected model reports as supported.

Can I keep a consistent brand style?＋

Yes. Brand Kit stores your visual direction, colors and campaign language and can apply them to prompts and projects.

TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Policies

Understand how this preview handles your work, your account and payments.

Local preview disclosures

These describe the current product. Final business terms, refund rules and the legal operator’s details must be supplied before launch.

Privacy & dataPayments & refundsResponsible useAccounts & security
01 / PRIVACY

Your work stays on this device

Project details, prompt drafts, preferences and storyboard data are saved in this browser. Imported media is stored in its local media database. Clearing browser data can remove these items.

The local account server stores email account records and password hashes on this computer. Signing in does not currently synchronize your browser’s projects or media to another device.

Opening the studio requests a public model catalog through the server. Generation is not connected. When a generation integration is enabled, prompts and chosen references will need to be sent to the selected provider.

02 / PAYMENTS

No charges in this preview

Plans and token packs are proposed product offers. Checkout is not connected, no payment card details are collected, and displayed tokens are not a purchased balance.

Before purchases can open, the operator must publish renewal, cancellation, taxes, token expiration and refund terms. No refund timetable is promised in this preview.

03 / RESPONSIBLE USE

Create with permission

Only use media you own or have permission to use. Obtain consent before using a person’s likeness or voice. Do not create deceptive impersonations, exploitative content or material that violates others’ rights.

Provider rules and applicable law also apply. Commercial rights and content restrictions depend on the selected provider and the material you supply. Final service terms are pending.

04 / SECURITY

A clear boundary for private information

Password hashing and account sessions are handled by the server. Provider keys, payment credentials and Google OAuth secrets belong on the server, never in the browser.

Google sign-in needs the operator’s OAuth configuration. Password-reset email, verified production accounts, durable sessions and account deletion require further services before launch.
